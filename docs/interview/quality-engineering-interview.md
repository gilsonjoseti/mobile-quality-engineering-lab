# Quality Engineering Interview Guide

## Core principles
- Shift-left testing
- Risk-based prioritization
- Observability and evidence
- CI/CD integration
- Quality gates and metrics

## Why it matters
Quality engineering is not just automated execution; it is the systematic design of reliable, measurable, and business-aligned validation across the lifecycle.
